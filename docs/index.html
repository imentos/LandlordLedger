Auto-assign transactions to the right property.

RentalKeeper uses local rules on your phone to match imported transactions. Uncertain items stay visible for review instead of disappearing into a black box.

1

Tenant names

Zelle, QuickPay, and ACH rent credits can match tenant names and aliases, then assign the tenant's property.

2

Expected rent

If one tenant has a matching monthly rent amount, RentalKeeper can suggest that tenant and property.

3

Property keywords

Descriptions containing a property name, address, owner, or custom keyword can map to that property.

4

Mortgage aliases

Lender names like Rocket Mortgage or Chase Home Lending can map mortgage payments to the correct property.

Questions landlords ask.

Does RentalKeeper connect to my bank?

No. RentalKeeper does not require bank login. You import CSV files or paste CSV text when you want to add transactions.

Which transactions can be auto-assigned?

Rent payments can match tenant names, aliases, and expected rent amounts. Expenses can match property names, addresses, owners, keywords, and mortgage lender aliases.

Can I review or fix a wrong match?

Yes. Open the transaction detail screen and choose the correct property, tenant, and category.

Is this tax advice?

No. RentalKeeper is an organizational tool. Review all records with your tax, legal, or accounting professional before filing.
